## Claim

**2026-10-22** — Small producers registering for the 2027 reporting year must pay a registration fee of £1,303 if they register directly with their environmental regulator, or £696 if they register through a compliance scheme. Those using a compliance scheme must also pay fees to the scheme for its services.

> You must register and pay a registration fee each year. For small producers to register for the 2027 reporting year, the fee is:
- £1,303 if you register directly with your environmental regulator
- £696 if you register through a compliance scheme
You’ll also need to pay fees to the compliance scheme if you choose to use one.

Issues:
- The 2027 registration deadline is misstated: the source says small producers must submit their 2027 registration application by 1 October 2026. The 1 April 2027 date applies only if their sole packaging activity is selling filled packaging directly to an end user.
- The key date claiming a 2026-04-01 deadline for the 2026 registration application is not supported; the source says the deadline for 2026 was 1 October 2025.
- The 2027-04-01 key date conflates the correct reporting deadline for January to December 2026 packaging data with a general 2027 registration deadline, which is not 1 April 2027 for all small producers.
- The next-step entry stating that 1 April 2027 is the deadline for small producers to submit their 2027 registration application is unsupported except for the narrow end-user sale exception.
