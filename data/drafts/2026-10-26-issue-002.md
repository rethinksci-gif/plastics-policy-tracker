## In this issue

- The European Commission opened a public consultation on EU rules on biobased feedstock in plastic packaging, with feedback open from 21 October 2026 to 13 January 2027.
- The Commission opened a consultation on bio-based plastic packaging whose results will inform an assessment of the technological development and environmental performance of bio-based plastic packaging.
- The EU Public Procurement Regulation procedure file lists meetings with interest representatives, including Fairtrade Labelling Organizations International e.V., Upphandlingsmyndigheten and LO Sverige.

## What changed

## European Union

### [PPWR](/plastics-policy-tracker/dossiers/eu-ppwr/)

**What happened (2026-10-21):** The European Commission opened a public consultation on EU rules on biobased feedstock in plastic packaging (reference Ares(2026)9620334), with feedback open from 21 October 2026 to 13 January 2027. The initiative is listed as ACTIVE with front-end stage OPC_LAUNCHED and a foreseen act type of PROP_REG.

- **Stage:** no change (`in-application`)
- **Affects:** recyclers, converters, brand-owners, retailers, importers
- **Next:** Feedback period on the initiative closes (feedbackEndDate: 2027/01/13 23:59:59). (2027-01-13)
- **Source:** [https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/19498_en](https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/19498_en) · review [`2026-10-22-001`](/plastics-policy-tracker/reviews/2026-10-22-001/)

<aside class="analysis"><strong>Analysis</strong> (our interpretation, not part of the verified record): Analysis: a future instrument on biobased feedstock requirements could add feedstock-related documentation and verification duties alongside existing recycled-content rules, so converters and brand owners may need to track biobased content claims in packaging. Recyclers could face additional competition or complementarity questions where biobased feedstock is counted towards packaging targets.</aside>
