## In this issue

- The European Commission opened a feedback period on a delegated regulation establishing a list of non-OECD countries authorised to import certain non-hazardous waste from the EU, and the new Waste Shipment Regulation bans the export of non-hazardous waste to non-OECD countries from 21 May 2027.
- The UK published draft Regulations and a draft reference document on chemical recycling and adoption of a mass balance approach for Plastic Packaging Tax for technical consultation on 6 October 2026.
- The feedback period for the planned PPWR implementing act on rules for the calculation and verification of recycled content in plastic packaging ran from 14 August 2026 to 16 September 2026.
- A public feedback period on the PPWR delegated act establishing sustainability criteria for plastic recycling technologies ran from 14 August 2026 to 16 September 2026 and is closed.

## United Kingdom

### [UK Plastic Packaging Tax](/plastics-policy-tracker/dossiers/uk-plastic-packaging-tax/)

**What happened (2026-10-06):** On 6 October 2026, draft Regulations and a draft reference document on chemical recycling and adoption of a mass balance approach for Plastic Packaging Tax were published for technical consultation. The draft Regulations set out the conditions that businesses must meet to use a mass balance approach to account for chemically recycled plastic from 1 April 2027, including certification, auditing, monitoring and record-keeping requirements.

- **Stage:** no change (`in-application`)
- **Affects:** converters, importers, brand-owners, recyclers
- **Next:** None stated in the source.
- **Source:** [https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/draft-legislation-plastic-packaging-tax-chemical-recycling-and-adoption-of-a-mass-balance-approach](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/draft-legislation-plastic-packaging-tax-chemical-recycling-and-adoption-of-a-mass-balance-approach) · review [`2026-10-14-013`](/plastics-policy-tracker/reviews/2026-10-14-013/)

<details><summary>Source quote</summary>

> The draft Regulations set out the conditions that businesses must meet if they wish to use a mass balance approach to account for chemically recycled plastic for Plastic Packaging Tax (PPT) purposes from 1 April 2027. The draft reference document sets out the methodology for applying dual-use factors where required. Both should be read alongside the relevant provisions of the Finance Act 2021, as amended by the Finance Act 2026, in particular sections 49, 49A and 49B.

</details>

<aside class="analysis"><strong>Analysis</strong> (our interpretation, not part of the verified record): This may require recyclers and converters using chemically recycled plastic to obtain certification and comply with mass-balance audit and record-keeping rules before accounting for it for Plastic Packaging Tax purposes from 1 April 2027.</aside>
