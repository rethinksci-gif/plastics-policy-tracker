## In this issue

- The government responded to its consultation on potential certification for mechanically recycled plastic packaging, which received 116 responses. It intends to introduce mandatory certification for mechanically recycled plastic packaging from 1 April 2030, and will review certification and market readiness closer to implementation.
- Guidance on waste disposal fees for large producers under EPR for packaging states that the fees are based on local authorities’ waste management costs and depend on the weight and type of household packaging reported. It states that for 2026 data onwards the fee for each packaging material is adjusted (‘modulated’) on the basis of how recyclable it is, in line with the recyclability assessment methodology (RAM).

### [UK packaging EPR](/plastics-policy-tracker/dossiers/uk-pepr/)

**What happened (2026-10-28):** Guidance on waste disposal fees for large producers under EPR for packaging states that the fees are based on local authorities’ waste management costs and depend on the weight and type of household packaging reported. It states that for 2026 data onwards the fee for each packaging material is adjusted (‘modulated’) on the basis of how recyclable it is, in line with the recyclability assessment methodology (RAM).

- **Stage:** This update does not assert a legal-status change.
- **Next:** None stated in the source.
- **Source:** [https://www.gov.uk/guidance/waste-disposal-fees-for-large-producers-under-epr-for-packaging](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/waste-disposal-fees-for-large-producers-under-epr-for-packaging) · review [`2026-11-03-002`](/plastics-policy-tracker/reviews/2026-11-03-002/)

<details><summary>Source quote</summary>

> Packaging waste disposal fees are based on local authorities’ waste management costs. Your fee depends on the weight and type of household packaging you report. For 2026 data onwards, the fee for each packaging material is adjusted (referred to as ‘modulated’) based on how recyclable it is, in line with the recyclability assessment methodology (RAM):

</details>

<aside class="analysis"><strong>Analysis</strong> (our interpretation, not part of the verified record): Analysis: fee modulation from 2026 data onwards means packaging rated green under the RAM should attract a lower waste disposal fee than red-rated formats, which may steer converters and brand owners towards more recyclable specifications.</aside>
